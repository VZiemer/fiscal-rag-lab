Fiscal RAG Lab

Document retrieval with provenance, lexical search, custom vector indexes and read-only MCP tools.

Does not provide tax advice, tax calculations or NF-e issuance. The corpus combines official NCM/CFOP tables, CONFAZ acts and selected ICMS regulations from São Paulo and Minas Gerais. Coverage is partial, without a complete history or automatic update guarantee. Unverified validity remains unknown.

Connection

HTTPS endpoint: /mcp. Service status · Privacy

  • search_regulations: retrieve evidence and sources, with state and date filters.
  • get_cfop and get_ncm: look up codes in loaded tables, preserving notes, hierarchy and provenance.
  • explain_rule: retrieve evidence by identifier.

The vector control uses local lexical hashing; this deployment does not run a language model or LoRA training.

Examples: “Look up CFOP 5102”, “Look up NCM 0901.21.00” or “Search for tax substitution in Minas Gerais”. Always check the returned official reference, edition and limitations. Counts and hash of the deployed edition.
